videolan.org · redesign proposal

Three directions for the home of VLC.

Each option is a complete, static rebuild of videolan.org, with the same content and three very different ideas of what the site could be. Pick one, then switch between them from any page with the A · B · C control at the bottom.

Option B

Player

The website is VLC itself. You browse it the way you use the player you already know.

  • Classic menu bar: Media, Playback, Video, Subtitle, Tools, View, Help
  • Playlist sidebar, a seek bar that scrubs the page, VLC hotkeys
  • “Open Media” search, Media Information, switchable skins

Option C

Almanac

An editorial broadsheet that treats 30 years of VLC as a story worth reading.

  • Masthead, numbered chapters, front page
  • A wall of every format, a 27-year news timeline
  • Serif long-form reading, day and night editions

The whole site, carried over

Downloads, release notes since 1.1, security bulletins, press, events since 2002, developer libraries, VLMa docs and skin editor help, in every option.

Donations in one click

A full-screen checkout: Apple Pay, Google Pay, card and SEPA through VideoLAN’s Stripe checkout, PayPal one-time or monthly, bank transfer and Bitcoin.

Honest about the edges

Wiki, forums, GitLab and add-ons aren’t redesigned yet. Links to them show a short notice before leaving.
