Home/Features

The most complete feature-set

Video, audio, subtitles, streams. All of it.

VLC handles video, subtitle synchronisation, video filters and audio filters, across almost every format ever made. Expand a category to see the full list.

Hardware decoding on most platforms. Supports 0-copy on the GPU and falls back on software when required.

New in 3.0.24: FFmpeg 8.1, ATRAC3 and ATRAC9 decoding, CEA-708 captions in MP4, SRT listener mode, SFTP public-key authentication, and over 130 security fixes.

Customise with skins, build your own in the VLC skin editor, and add extensions.

Home/About

VideoLAN, a project and a non-profit organisation

Volunteers who believe in open source.

The VideoLAN project is led by a team of volunteers who believe in the power of open source to rock the multimedia world. It is supported by the VideoLAN non-profit organisation, based in France.

History

From a campus network to the whole world.

VideoLAN produces free software for multimedia, released under the General Public License, with developers from 40 countries.

1996

A student project

Started at the French École Centrale Paris to stream video across the campus network.

1998

A complete rewrite

The code base is rebuilt from the ground up.

2001

Open source under the GPL

Thanks to the agreement of École Centrale Paris, the project opens to developers worldwide.

2009

An independent non-profit

The project becomes fully separate from the school, driven by an autonomous non-profit organisation.

2024

Dev Days in South Korea

Multimedia hackers from around the globe meet for two days of technical talks.

2026

VLC 3.0.24

FFmpeg 8.1, 49 updated libraries and more than 130 security fixes.

Home/Design notes

About this proposal

A modern videolan.org that still loads on a 2009 netbook.

This concept keeps the content, tone and values of today’s site, and rebuilds the presentation as one small static HTML5 bundle that any volunteer can edit.

~90KBlt;40KBTotal transfer, gzipped. No images, no web fonts.
0Third-party requests, cookies or analytics
81Languages ready in the selector, with right-to-left support
AAWCAG 2.2 contrast, keyboard and screen-reader support

Static and lightweight

Three files: HTML, CSS and a small vanilla script. No framework, no build step, no database. Host it on any mirror. Without JavaScript every page still renders as one long, readable document.

Accessible to everyone

Semantic landmarks, a skip link, visible focus, 44px touch targets, reduced-motion support, Windows high-contrast mode, and a settings panel for text size, contrast, link underlines and reading spacing.

Built for 81 languages

The selector lists each language in its own script, with search and right-to-left layout for Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Urdu and Pashto. Only English is written so far; the structure is ready for translators.

Smarter downloads

The main button detects Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS and points to the right build. Every other system is one tap away, and checksum verification is promoted instead of hidden.

Light and dark

Follows the system setting by default and remembers your choice. Colours keep the famous orange, warm and recognisable, with darker tones for text so contrast stays above AA.

Respectful of the legacy

The cone, the orange, “Simple, fast and powerful”, “Plays everything”, the volunteer spirit and the no-tracking promise all stay front and centre. Mockups are pure CSS, so they never go stale like screenshots.
